# JotCue tester guide

Thank you for helping us test JotCue. Please use the same Google account whose email was added to the tester list.

## Get started

1. Open the testing link you receive while signed into the invited Google account.
2. Join the test, install JotCue from Google Play, and open it.
3. Sign in and allow notification permission when asked.

## What to test

1. **Create and organise:** Add, edit, search for, and delete a note. Create a checklist or task and mark an item complete. Try attaching a photo.
2. **Try natural reminders:** Type phrases such as:
   - "Remind me to drink water every 30 minutes."
   - "I need to drink water regularly." Then choose how often.
   - "Next week, I need to rearrange my calendar."
   - "Remind me next Monday at 6pm."
3. **Try the mini assistant:** Write a short multi-step plan on separate lines and check whether JotCue offers to turn it into tasks and suggests sensible reminders.
4. **Check alerts:** Set a reminder a few minutes ahead. Lock or leave the app and confirm that the alert appears on time with sound, vibration, and a pop-up, according to your phone settings.
5. **Test alarm handoff on Android:** Type "I need an alarm for an hour from now," tap **Open Clock**, check the proposed time, and confirm it in the Clock app. Remove the test alarm afterward.
6. **Try everyday use:** Close and reopen JotCue, use it on both Wi-Fi and mobile data, and check that your notes remain available.

## Send feedback

Please report:

- what you were trying to do;
- what you expected to happen;
- what actually happened;
- your phone model and Android version;
- a screenshot or screen recording, if possible.

Please do not include passwords or private information in test notes or screenshots.
