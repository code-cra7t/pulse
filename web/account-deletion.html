Delete your PulseNotes account

You can complete account deletion from a phone, tablet, desktop app, or the PulseNotes web app.

  1. Open PulseNotes and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Under Account, select Delete account.
  4. Read the warning, confirm your password, and select Delete permanently.
Open the PulseNotes web app

What is deleted

Your PulseNotes account, profile, notes, tasks, reminders, settings, uploaded images, and local PulseNotes note cache are permanently deleted. Scheduled PulseNotes notifications on the current device are canceled.

Before you continue

Deletion cannot be undone. You must be able to sign in and confirm the account password. If you cannot access your account, use the developer contact shown on the PulseNotes Google Play listing.
